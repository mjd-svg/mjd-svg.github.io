Remote: Privacy Policy

Developer: Mert00 · App: app.pocket.remote · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information Remote (“the app”) uses and why. In short: the app has no accounts and we run no servers, so we never receive your personal information. What you create in the app stays on your phone.

Information kept on your phone

Your progress, settings and anything you make in the app are stored only on your device. Android may include them in your own device backup if you have backups turned on. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app is free and shows banner ads, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, your interactions with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash logs. This data is encrypted in transit.

Ads may be personalised to your interests. Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws) the app first asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from the “Ad privacy” link under the banner. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

You can buy “Remove ads” once through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it is restored on a new phone.

Permissions

  • Wi-Fi state: to find devices on your local network.
  • Network access: for the app's own features on your local network.
  • Infrared: to send remote-control signals from your phone's IR blaster.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and Google Play purchase handling described above, the app shares no data with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off reminders and notifications in the app or in Android Settings, change ad consent from “Ad privacy”, reset your advertising ID, and delete all app data by clearing it in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
